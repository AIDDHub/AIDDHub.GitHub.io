About

The developer behind AIDDHub: two desktop programs for medicinal chemistry, this blog, and the reasons for both.

I am the developer behind AIDDHub. One person, two desktop programs, and this blog.

What I do

I build MolStudio, a modelling and docking workstation, and MolInsight, an SAR analysis workbench. Both are native Windows and Linux applications that run offline, are sold once, and are meant to be useful without an account.

I write the code, the documentation, the installer, the support replies and the posts here. That is not a humblebrag; it is the reason the scope stays small and the reason some obvious features are still missing.

Why this blog exists

Product pages are bad at nuance. They cannot explain why a docking score under −8 is not proof of anything, or why a matched-pair p-value with three pairs is decoration, because saying so is not a sales argument.

So the writing lives here instead: walkthroughs that show the whole workflow including the parts that fail, build logs about decisions I got wrong the first time, and occasional essays about software for chemistry. The tutorials are the practical half; the notes are the part I would write anyway.

Everything published here is checked against the current release. When a feature changes, the article changes with it. When I was wrong, I say so and leave the correction visible.

How I work

  • Local first. Structures, assay tables and models stay on your machine. Nothing is uploaded to be analysed, because there is no server to upload it to.
  • One-time licences. No subscription. A free tier that can finish real work, with no time limit and no watermark on the output.
  • Windows and Linux. Native installers. No Java runtime, no conda environment, no administrator rights for a per-user install.
  • English and Chinese. The interface, the documentation and this site exist in both.

What this is not

It is not a company with a sales team, a partnership programme or an enterprise edition. There is no admin console and no SSO, because each of those is a product in its own right and pretending otherwise with a checkbox wastes a buyer's afternoon.

If you need ten seats, you buy ten licences. That is the whole story.

Getting in touch

Bug reports, corrections, feature requests and licence questions all go to support@aiddhub.com. I read everything and answer in English or Chinese, usually within a couple of days.

If something on this site is wrong, telling me is genuinely useful — a correction costs you two minutes and saves the next reader an afternoon.

The products live at www.aiddhub.com.
