About

Computational chemistry: molecular modelling, docking and structure–activity analysis — and the notes that come out of it.

I work on computational chemistry: molecular modelling, docking, and structure–activity analysis. Most of what is published here comes out of that work — methods I use, tools I compare, and notes on the parts that took longer than expected.

What is here

  • Tutorials — complete walkthroughs, from a real task to a figure or table that belongs in a report.
  • Writing — essays, build notes and opinions on software and method.
  • Projects — the tools I build and maintain.

Background

My work sits between medicinal chemistry and software: docking and interaction analysis on one side, data handling, plotting and statistics on the other. A lot of the notes here exist because the same question came up more than once, and writing it down was cheaper than answering it again.

How the writing is done

Articles are tested against the current release of whatever they describe. When something changes, the article changes with it. When an earlier statement turns out to be wrong, the correction stays visible instead of being quietly deleted.

Contact

Corrections, questions and suggestions: support@aiddhub.com. English or Chinese.

If something on this site is wrong, telling me is useful — a correction costs two minutes and saves the next reader an afternoon.
