About

AIDDHub is an independent software studio building desktop tools for molecular modelling and SAR analysis.

AIDDHub is an independent software studio. We build desktop software for medicinal chemistry and structural biology, and publish the notes that come out of that work.

What we build

MolStudio is a molecular modelling and docking workstation: 3D building, receptor preparation, docking with AutoDock Vina, 2D interaction diagrams, plots and saved sessions.

MolInsight is an SAR analysis workbench: 47 chart types, matched molecular pairs with pair counts and significance, R-group matrices, scaffold and side-chain analysis, clustering and self-organising maps, and QSAR with an applicability domain.

Both are native Windows and Linux applications. They run offline, keep data in ordinary files, and are sold as one-time licences with a free tier that has no time limit.

How these notes are written

Every article is tested against the current release before it is published. When a feature changes, the article changes with it. When an earlier statement turns out to be wrong, the correction stays visible instead of being quietly deleted.

The tutorials are practical: a real task, the actual steps, and a figure or table at the end. The writing is more open-ended — design decisions, comparisons, and the parts of the work that do not fit on a product page.

What we do not do

No subscription, no seat management, no admin console, no SSO, no cloud upload. A laboratory that needs ten seats buys ten licences. Anything else would be a second product to support, and a worse one.

Contact

Bug reports, corrections, feature requests and licence questions go to support@aiddhub.com. English or Chinese; we answer within a couple of days.

If something on this site is wrong, telling us is useful — a correction costs two minutes and saves the next reader an afternoon.
