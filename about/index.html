I work on computational chemistry — molecular modelling, docking and structure–activity analysis — and on the software that makes that work less manual.

What I work on

Modelling and docking. Preparing structures properly, running the search, and reading the interactions rather than only the score.

SAR analysis. Matched pairs, R-groups, clustering, and the statistics that keep the conclusions honest.

Tooling. The scripts, plots and desktop programs that hold a workflow together when it has to run again next month.

Writing

Two kinds of posts. Tutorials walk through a complete task with the actual steps. Notes are essays, build notes and opinions — including the decisions that turned out wrong.

Everything is checked against the current release before it is published. When something changes, the article changes with it; corrections stay visible rather than being quietly removed.
